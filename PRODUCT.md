# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Next.js (App Router) + vanilla Three.js + GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis, Tailwind CSS. Deploy target: Vercel. Approved in PROJECT_BRIEF.md.

## Users
UK home buyers in the first launch area (London):
- The time-poor buyer: no time to scroll portals.
- The detail-oriented first-time buyer: needs to trust the data.
- The relocating family: searches by feel (schools, commute, quiet) in an area they don't know.

## Product Purpose
Property Scanner is AI property search. The buyer describes the home in plain words once, refines by chat, decides, and sends an enquiry. Landing page success: "Book a demo" requests.

## Positioning
Buyers think in feelings, portals think in filters. Property Scanner turns feelings into a scan of real listings, and it shows what's known while labelling what's guessed.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- First-area MVP: London only.
- Happy path: Describe → Refine by chat → Decide → Send.
- Demo form submissions are only logged (server console). No storage yet.

## Brand Commitments
- Colours: black and yellow (user-pinned).
- Fonts: Outfit (sans) and Newsreader (serif) (user-pinned).
- The landing page must use next-level Three.js 3D with heavy scroll-driven animation (user-pinned).

## Evidence on Hand
None. No customers, testimonials, metrics or pricing exist. Never fabricate them. Any listing or demo data shown on the page must be labelled illustrative.

## Product Principles
- Describe once, then refine. Never make the buyer fill a filter grid.
- Show what's known and label what's guessed.
- Speak like a buyer, not like a portal.
